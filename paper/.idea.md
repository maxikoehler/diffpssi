# Ideas for Optimization Application

> Using the optimization possibilities of diffpssi on discrete OLTC control schemes

## Possible Problem Statements and Targets

1. **Minimizing the number of switches**
after a disturbance e.g., LVRT after short-circuit or HVRT after loss of loads; while staying in a stable operation window
-> Optimized values would be the transfomer and its control parameters 
2. **Minimizing the voltage instability**
after a short-circuit event e.g., with use of an envelope (TVI) and the minimizing the time of last time leaving it or damping voltage swings with the area exceeded of the TVI 
-> Optimized values would be the transfomer and its control parameters

## Mathematical Formulations and Terms

| Symbol | Description |
|:-|:-|
|$\mathcal{N}$ | Set of nodes |
|$\mathcal{V}$ | Set of vertices (lines, transformers, , ...) |

Example formulation for problem 1, where
- $n_s$ is the number of switches, with $n_s \in \mathbb{N}$
- $V_\mathrm{band}$ is an enveloping function
- $v_\mathrm{db} \in [0,0.05]$ is the size of the dead-band, where no swithing occurs
- $\Delta m,\Delta k \in \mathbb{R}^+$ are the ratio per OLTC and FSM tap position change
- $n_\mathrm{viol}$ is the number of envelope violations in the time period $T$

$$
\begin{alignat}{3}
    \min_{n_s \in \mathbb{N}} \quad &n_s(\Delta m,\Delta k, v_\mathrm{db}), \quad&&v_\mathrm{db} \in [0,0.05];~\Delta m,\Delta k \in [0,2 \cdot v_\mathrm{db}]\\
    \text{s.t.} \quad& \underline{V}_\mathrm{band} \leq V_t \leq \overline{V}_\mathrm{band}, \quad&&\forall t \in T \\
    \quad&\text{TVI} \leq \epsilon \\
    % \quad&n_\mathrm{viol} \leq n_\mathrm{max}
\end{alignat}
$$
